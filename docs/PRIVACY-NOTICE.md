# Spongecake staff privacy notice

Spongecake is Woodhead Restaurant Group's internal recipe app. This notice
explains what it stores about you.

**What we hold.** Your name, your work email address, which sites you belong to
and your role there, the recipes you mark as favourites, and the recipes and
suggestions you write or edit with your name and the time. The app also keeps,
on your own phone only, which recipes you opened recently and your light/dark
preference.

**Why.** To let you sign in, to show who wrote or changed a recipe, and to run
the app. We use it for nothing else. We don't track where you are or how often
you use it, and we don't share it outside the group.

**Sign-in emails** are sent through Resend on our behalf. Your email address is
used only to send the code.

**Where it lives.** On servers in London, with Vercel and Neon.

**How long.** While you work for the group. When you leave, your account is
removed. Your name stays on recipes you wrote or changed so the kitchen knows
where they came from.

**Your rights.** You can see what we hold by opening your profile in the app.
Ask James (james@woodheadrestaurantgroup.co.uk) if you want anything corrected
or removed.
